## Design Context

### Users
VoyageAtlas is for travelers who want to record trips with minimal friction and relive them as spatial stories on a 3D globe. They use the app to add journeys, inspect routes, filter parts of their travel history, and share the resulting route experience.

### Brand Personality
Immersive, elegant, and exploratory. The interface should feel like a precise travel command deck: cinematic enough to make memories feel alive, but quiet enough that the globe remains the hero.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, atmospheric, globe-first UI with refined glass panels, restrained cyan/seafoam light, warm route accents, compact controls, and strong legibility. Avoid generic dashboard clutter, oversized marketing layouts, and UI that competes with the globe.

### Design Principles
- Keep the 3D globe as the first visual signal and primary interaction surface.
- Make journey registration and playback controls quick, visible, and low-friction.
- Prefer compact, purposeful panels over decorative cards.
- Use motion and color to clarify travel state, not as noise.
- Preserve readable, touch-safe controls across desktop and mobile.
